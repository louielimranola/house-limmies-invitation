Long before the Seven Kingdoms bent the knee — before dragons turned to ash and memory — there were Houses.

Houses bound not by blood alone, but by fire.

Across the Narrow Sea, in a hall lit by dragonfire, House Lomar has kept its word for a thousand years.

Fire cannot kill a dragon.

And it cannot kill what House Lomar feels for you.

A raven has been sent.

Not with war. Not with tribute.

But with an invitation.

The Lord of House Lomar summons you to the table —

to break bread beneath the same roof, on a day of your choosing.
